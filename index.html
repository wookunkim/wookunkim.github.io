Wookun Kim

Assistant Professor of Economics

Southern Methodist University

I go by Woo.

I am an applied microeconomist whose research integrates family and urban economics.

I study how economic conditions and public policy shape migration, education, and childbearing, and how demographic change reshapes labor markets and inequality across places. I assemble data from multiple sources and use quasi-experimental designs and economic models to identify causal effects and evaluate consequences for earnings, health, and household welfare.
