Wookun Kim

Assistant Professor of Economics

Southern Methodist University

My research connects economic geography with labor markets and demographic change.

I study how public policy and technological change shape where people live and work, how they invest in education, and the decisions they make about their families. My work combines empirical evidence with economic models to understand these adjustments and their implications for inequality and well-being.
