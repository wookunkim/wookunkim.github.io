Teaching & mentoring

I want students to leave my courses able to think independently, reason carefully, and use economics to ask better questions about the world.

Teaching materials

Teaching statement↗Evidence of teaching effectiveness↗

My teaching combines economic theory, empirical analysis, and discussion. I ask students to move beyond applying a familiar formula: to articulate a mechanism, examine the evidence, and explain what a result does—and does not—establish. I see teaching as an evolving practice, shaped by student feedback, my own assessment, and the skills the world demands.

Mentoring

My goal is to help students develop the confidence and intellectual independence to become thoughtful leaders and scholars. In research and project work, I emphasize asking substantive questions, making transparent analytical choices, and communicating clearly.

Learning with AI

Students may use AI to augment and enrich their learning, not to replace it. I emphasize the responsibility to direct these tools thoughtfully, evaluate their output, and remain accountable for the reasoning and work they submit.
